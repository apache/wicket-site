Upgrade paths

How to move an application to a line that stays supported.

You are on Your route Guide
Wicket 8.x or 9.x

8.x → 9 (Java 11) → 10 LTS (Java 17)

Both lines get one final release and then reach end of life, and with them Wicket's support for javax.servlet. Move to 10 LTS: it brings jakarta.servlet and is supported until July 2027. 9.x moves to 10 directly. 8.x takes the Wicket 9 step first, because each migration guide covers one major version.

For the step to 10, the OpenRewrite recipes of wicket-migration do the repetitive part: the jakarta imports, the Java 17 baseline and the moved Wicket classes. If you need help or more time, companies that offer Wicket support are listed on the wiki.

Release policy

Wicket 10 is the current long-term support (LTS) release. It receives security fixes and applicable bug fixes until July 2027. Wicket 8.x and 9.x each receive one final release and then reach end of life, and with them Wicket’s support for the javax.servlet API.

What end of life means for 8 and 9, and how to move, is in Wicket 8 and 9 reach end of life soon. The release schedule after that, with its new release model, is in A new release cadence for Apache Wicket.

End of life

These release lines get no more releases, not even security fixes. If your application depends on one of them, upgrade to Wicket 10 LTS; the upgrade paths show the route.
