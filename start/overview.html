On wide screens with JavaScript one step shows at a time and the rail of beads at the top chooses it; javascript/ribbon.js grows the ribbon into what is new. Without JavaScript and on narrow screens the steps follow each other, each tree as a list with its files, and the rail is their table of contents. {% endcomment %} {% assign steps = site.data.how.steps %}
